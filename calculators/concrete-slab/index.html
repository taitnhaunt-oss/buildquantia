Before you order

This tool provides a planning estimate. Product coverage, packaging, site conditions, waste and local requirements can change the final amount.

Tip: keep your measurements in feet/inches as shown, and use the waste field when the project has cuts, irregular edges or handling loss.
ABOUT THIS TOOL

How the estimate works

Measure a slab and turn length, width, and depth into a practical concrete order estimate. The calculator shows the base measurement first, then uses your waste or product-coverage inputs to turn that measurement into a practical ordering quantity.

Is this an exact quote?

No. It is an estimating tool. Always compare the result with the product's stated coverage and your actual site conditions.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. The calculator is designed to work on current desktop and mobile browsers.
