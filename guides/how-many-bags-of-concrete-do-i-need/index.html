How Many Bags of Concrete Do I Need?

Estimate the number of concrete bags from a required volume and the yield printed on your product.

Start with the measurements

Measure the part of the project that will receive the material. Keep units consistent, write down the dimensions, and use the calculator linked below to avoid manual conversions.

Allow for real-world waste

Cuts, irregular edges, handling loss and site conditions can mean the theoretical quantity is not the same as the amount you should order. BuildQuantia lets you enter a waste allowance on tools that use one.

Check the product you plan to buy

Coverage varies by product. Before ordering, compare the calculator result with the manufacturer's stated coverage, package size and any project-specific guidance.
