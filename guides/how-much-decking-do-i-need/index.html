How Much Decking Do I Need?

Estimate decking material from deck area, board coverage and waste.

Measure first

Take the dimensions of the area or structure you are planning. Record the unit shown in the calculator so you do not accidentally mix feet, inches or product coverage units.

Plan for the material you will actually buy

The mathematical quantity and the shopping quantity are not always identical. Cuts, openings, irregular shapes and package sizes can change the final order. Use the calculator to test a realistic allowance.

Verify the product

Coverage and package sizes differ. Check the label or manufacturer's documentation before ordering and treat the result as an estimate rather than a guaranteed quantity.
